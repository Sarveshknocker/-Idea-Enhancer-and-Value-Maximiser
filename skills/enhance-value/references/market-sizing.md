# Bottom-up market sizing

## Formula
**Revenue potential = number of reachable buyers × expected adoption × annual price (value metric × volume)**

| Layer | Definition | Example (fill with sourced numbers) |
|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | Every buyer who has the problem, worldwide, at your price | # smallholder dairy cooperatives worldwide × annual price |
| **SAM** | Buyers you can serve with your product, channel, language and regulation | Cooperatives in 3 Indian states with smartphone-using managers |
| **SOM** | Realistic 3-year capture given team, channel and competition | SAM × 2–5 % adoption (justify with comparable adoption curves) |

## Rules
- Every input has a source (grade A/B preferred) or is labelled **assumption** with a range (low / base / high).
- Count **buyers**, not users, when the payer differs.
- Include a sanity check: "how many sales per month does SOM imply, and can our channel produce that?"
- Show a sensitivity line: which single assumption changes the result most?
