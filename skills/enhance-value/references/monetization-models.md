# Monetization models library

| Model | How it earns | Best when | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subscription (SaaS)** | Recurring fee per seat/account | Ongoing workflow value, B2B | Churn; must deliver value every month |
| **Usage-based** | Per API call, transaction, GB, shipment, message | Value scales with usage; variable customers | Revenue unpredictability; bill shock |
| **Freemium** | Free core, paid advanced/limits | Viral/self-serve products with low marginal cost | Free tier too generous → no upgrades |
| **Tiered packaging** | Good/Better/Best + Enterprise | Heterogeneous segments | Too many tiers confuse |
| **Take rate (marketplace)** | % of GMV between two sides | Fragmented supply & demand, trust gaps | Disintermediation; chicken-and-egg |
| **Outcome / success fee** | Paid only when a result happens (savings, recovered revenue, hires) | Measurable outcomes, risk-averse buyers | Attribution disputes; cash-flow lag |
| **Hardware + service (HaaS)** | Device sale/lease + recurring data/insights | Physical-world problems (IoT, health, agri) | Capex, logistics, support |
| **Transaction/embedded finance** | Payment fees, credit, insurance on top of the workflow | You sit where money moves | Regulation (licensing, KYC) |
| **Data & insights licensing** | Aggregated, anonymised insights to third parties | Unique aggregated data | Privacy law, consent, trust |
| **API / platform** | Developers pay to build on you | Clear reusable capability | Needs dev-rel and docs |
| **Open-core** | Free OSS + paid hosting/enterprise features | Developer tools, infra | Cloud providers competing on your OSS |
| **Managed service** | Done-for-you with software leverage | Customers lack skills/time | Margins; scaling people |
| **Licensing / white-label** | Others brand and resell | Enterprises/partners own distribution | Less brand control |
| **Sponsorship / B2B2C** | A third party pays for users (employer, insurer, brand, government, CSR) | Users can't pay but others benefit | Dependence on few sponsors |
| **Grants & public funding** | Non-dilutive funding for impact | Social/climate/health/agri impact | Slow, restricted use |
| **Community / membership** | Paid community, events, certification | Identity-driven audiences | Needs continuous content |
| **Certification / compliance** | Fee to be verified/badged | Buyers need trust signals | Credibility must be real |
| **Lead generation / affiliate** | Referral fees from providers | High-intent audience | Conflicts of interest; disclose |

## Premium levers (what people pay more for)
Time saved · risk removed (compliance, insurance, guarantees) · money made (revenue uplift) · status & identity · certainty (SLAs, predictions) · convenience (done-for-you) · exclusivity/access · integration into existing systems · priority/human support · white-label & customisation.

## Pricing heuristics
- Anchor to the **value created**, not your cost. Capture 10–30 % of it.
- Price the **value metric** customers already think in.
- Annual plans with a discount (15–20 %) improve cash flow and retention.
- Local purchasing power: regional pricing (e.g. INR tiers, micro-payments via UPI, sachet pricing).
- Test prices early (landing-page pricing tests, pre-orders, paid pilots).
