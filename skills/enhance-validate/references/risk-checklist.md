# Risk checklist

## Desirability
- [ ] Evidence that the pain is frequent and severe (grade A/B), not just "nice to have"
- [ ] The target segment is reachable through a named channel
- [ ] The new behaviour asked of users is small (one new habit at most)

## Viability
- [ ] A named payer with budget/authority; willingness-to-pay evidence
- [ ] Unit economics plausible (LTV:CAC ≥ 3, payback ≤ 18 months) or a credible path
- [ ] Not dependent on a single customer, sponsor, platform or API
- [ ] Pricing survives competitor undercutting

## Feasibility
- [ ] Core tech exists today (open source / APIs / proven research), not "needs a breakthrough"
- [ ] Data needed is obtainable legally and with consent
- [ ] Team can build v1 in ≤ 6 weeks with reuse
- [ ] Operations (hardware, support, logistics) scoped

## Legal & regulatory
- [ ] Personal data: lawful basis, consent, minimisation, retention, cross-border transfer (GDPR / DPDP / CCPA)
- [ ] Sector licensing: payments/lending/insurance, medical devices/health advice, food safety, drones/radio, education, employment
- [ ] Consumer protection: clear pricing, refund policy, no dark patterns
- [ ] Open-source licenses compatible with the business model (AGPL for SaaS, GPL for distributed binaries, attribution)
- [ ] Platform terms: WhatsApp Business policy, app store rules, API rate limits/ToS, scraping restrictions
- [ ] Trademarks/names checked

## Ethics & safety
- [ ] Could it harm users physically, financially or psychologically? Safeguards?
- [ ] Could it be misused (surveillance, fraud, discrimination)? Mitigations?
- [ ] AI features: human oversight, explainability, bias checks, clear disclosure
- [ ] Accessibility (language, literacy, disability)
- [ ] Environmental footprint (hardware waste, compute)
